# The Twelve Clans — launch sticker pack

Original fantasy setting and characters inspired by classic tabletop RPG archetypes. Each sticker family represents one of twelve clans: kinship houses bound by blood, adoption, shared craft or ancient oath. Evolution follows a clan champion from newcomer to living legend; rarity never changes. Three matching unlocked copies combine into one next-level copy. Level 3 duplicates remain collectible. Stable family IDs preserve existing collections and artwork when a clan's display name changes.

## Shared narrative

When the old empire's wishing coin split in two, its twelve sworn clans reclaimed the ancestral seals once surrendered to the imperial mint. Now every clan tells a different story of the breaking: betrayal, liberation, accident or prophecy. They contest the seals through champions, hoping to reunite the coin and decide what the next age will become. Every flip is an omen; every Flip Off settles a small feud in that greater struggle. Three echoes of a champion awaken the next form of their clan's legacy. Nobody knows whether the coin grants wishes or merely reveals what each clan is willing to wager.

## Common — five families

### 1. Hearthshield Clan
<!-- family-id: hearthshield-compact -->
Human hearth-kin who adopt anyone willing to guard a neighbor's door. They want the seals held in common, not inherited by rulers. Rounded shields, warm ochre cloaks, copper kettle emblem; friendly stout silhouettes. Rival: Crownless Clan, whose bargains once cost them their ancestral hall.
- Level 1 **Hearth Recruit** — oversized round shield, patched cloak, wooden practice spear. “Our clan begins wherever someone guards another's door.”
- Level 2 **Lantern Captain** — broad steel shield, lantern-topped spear, ember cape. “Let their banners darken the road. We brought our own light.”
- Level 3 **Citadel Warden** — tower-shaped shield and radiant lantern armor, still carrying the battered kettle crest. “Our walls have room for everyone their crowns forgot.”

### 2. Mosswhisper Clan
<!-- family-id: mosswhisper-circle -->
Woodland kin whose ancestral memories live in the roots beneath the ruined mint. They seek to grow a new age from its buried seals. Fern green, cream bark, curling antlers and leaf spirals; branching silhouettes. Rival: Brassburrow Clan, whose excavations disturb the ancestor grove.
- Level 1 **Seed Tender** — hooded sprite with acorn staff and tiny sprout companion. “They buried their fortune. We planted ours.”
- Level 2 **Briar Speaker** — bark mantle, bramble antlers, flowering staff. “Brassburrow hears buried gears. We hear our ancestors asking them to stop.”
- Level 3 **Grove Sovereign** — towering gentle tree spirit, crown of living branches. “When their last vault falls, we will call it a garden.”

### 3. Brassburrow Clan
<!-- family-id: brassburrow-guild -->
Dwarf forge-families who pass tools and unpaid imperial debts from parent to apprentice. They would reopen the mint so no clan works for another's crown. Brass and charcoal, square tools, gear badges; compact block silhouettes. Rival: Mosswhisper Clan, whose sacred roots have claimed their oldest workshop.
- Level 1 **Tunnel Tinker** — soot-faced dwarf, small wrench, brass mole lantern. “A lost empire is mostly overdue maintenance.”
- Level 2 **Vaultwright** — piston gauntlets, tool belt, reinforced apron. “Mosswhisper calls it sacred ground. Grandmother called it her workshop.”
- Level 3 **Deepforge Foreman** — squat walking forge suit, glowing hammer and smokestack shoulders. “We built the future. Kindly stop standing in the gears.”

### 4. Redcap Clan
<!-- family-id: redcap-runners -->
Goblin road-kin whose shared red scarves mark a family larger than any bloodline. They carry the twelve clans' messages and demand an equal voice at the new council. Scarlet scarves, patched tan leather, triangular ears, winged boot crest; nimble diagonals. Rival: Dawn Oath Clan, whose checkpoints threaten their free roads.
- Level 1 **Road Scamp** — goblin with enormous mail satchel and red cap. “First to the crossroads gets to name the price.”
- Level 2 **Banner Dasher** — windblown scarf, courier spear, bundle of stolen proclamations. “We carry every clan's word. Believing it costs extra.”
- Level 3 **Crossroads Marshal** — long red pennant, spring boots, road-map cape. “Kings own castles. We own the way there.”

### 5. Moonwake Clan
<!-- family-id: moonwake-fellowship -->
Frogfolk boat-families whose clan history is sung rather than written. They want the reunited coin to free every waterway from borders and tolls. Teal and pearl, crescent paddles and lily pads; soft oval silhouettes. Rival: Tideglass Clan, who guard a prophecy Moonwake believes belongs in every song.
- Level 1 **Reed Piper** — tiny frog bard, reed flute, lily-pad hat. “The river remembers a verse the kings left out.”
- Level 2 **Moonboat Skald** — crescent lute, reed cloak, little spectral boat. “We sing their borders into water.”
- Level 3 **Silverwater Maestro** — sweeping moonlit robes and luminous ribbon river. “Let twelve clans quarrel. The current carries all our echoes.”

## Uncommon — four families

### 6. Dawn Oath Clan
<!-- family-id: dawn-oath -->
An oath-bound clan of paladins who inherit a promise, not a throne: never let the wishing coin crown another tyrant. They would destroy it even if the other eleven clans stood against them. Ivory and sunrise gold, sunburst helms and long lances; upright angular silhouettes. Rival: Redcap Clan, whose messengers keep the dangerous seals moving.
- Level 1 **Sun Squire** — bright tabard, simple lance, small sunrise buckler. “I inherit no crown. Only the oath that broke one.”
- Level 2 **Daybreak Knight** — fan-shaped sun helm, white horsehair plume, gilded lance. “No wish is worth another chain.”
- Level 3 **Aurora Justiciar** — blazing halo shield, long flowing ivory mantle. “We did not come for the throne. We came to end its hunger.”

### 7. Velvetclaw Clan
<!-- family-id: velvet-knives -->
Catfolk shadow-families who welcome debtors and outcasts as kin. They steal seals to erase the imperial ledgers that still bind their adopted siblings. Plum and rose, velvet masks, curved daggers; sleek crescent silhouettes. Rival: Ashquill Clan, who refuse to burn a single ancestral record.
- Level 1 **Alley Whisper** — masked cat thief, lockpick and folded ledger. “A purse closes. A secret opens.”
- Level 2 **Silkblade Agent** — twin crescent daggers, layered purple sash. “Tonight we steal what tomorrow calls lawful.”
- Level 3 **Midnight Regent** — dramatic velvet cape, silver mask, orbiting stolen seals. “Ashquill keeps the names. We free the people still chained to them.”

### 8. Ashquill Clan
<!-- family-id: ashen-archive -->
Undead scholar-kin who remain beside their descendants to preserve every clan's history, including its betrayals. Their ancestral archive holds the last account of the coin's breaking. Ash gray and violet, paper strips and bone quills; tall narrow silhouettes. Rival: Velvetclaw Clan, whose liberating fires threaten memories as well as debts.
- Level 1 **Bone Scribe** — small skeleton in scholar robes, giant quill. “History is easier to keep when you need no sleep.”
- Level 2 **Cinder Curator** — floating librarian, scroll halo, violet candle. “Eleven clans ask who broke the coin. The twelfth tore out the page.”
- Level 3 **Sepulcher Sage** — skeletal archmage inside an open book-shaped mantle. “Erase a debt if you must. Do not erase who paid it.”

### 9. Stormhorn Clan
<!-- family-id: stormhorn-clans -->
Mountain minotaur kin who carve their ancestors' names into living horn-runes. They seek the seals to recover sky relics taken when their elders bent the knee to the empire. Cobalt and storm silver, horn runes and zigzag axes; broad triangular silhouettes. Rival: Emberwing Clan, who keep those relics beside the first furnace.
- Level 1 **Crag Runner** — young horned climber, stone axe, blue sash. “The thunder speaks the names our clan refuses to forget.”
- Level 2 **Tempest Reaver** — crackling rune horns, forked axe, storm cape. “We climbed above their walls to find a larger fight.”
- Level 3 **Skybreaker Chief** — massive silver horns, lightning axe and rolling cloud shoulders. “Return the sky, or learn how mountains answer.”

## Rare — two families

### 10. Tideglass Clan
<!-- family-id: tideglass-choir -->
Merfolk oracle-kin who pass visions between generations in singing shells. They saw a future for each clan, but the reunited coin may leave room for only one. Sea-glass turquoise and coral pink, translucent fins and hourglass shells; flowing fan silhouettes. Rival: Moonwake Clan, whose songs might reveal the future they are trying to prevent.
- Level 1 **Foam Listener** — small merfolk oracle holding a whispering shell. “One ripple told us the coin would fall again.”
- Level 2 **Pearl Diviner** — coral crown, suspended pearl, sweeping transparent fins. “Twelve clans entered the vision. One returned without a name.”
- Level 3 **Abyssal Cantor** — luminous deep-sea mantle, floating hourglass shell, starry fins. “We could tell you who wins. Then the song would change.”

### 11. Emberwing Clan
<!-- family-id: emberwing-covenant -->
Dragonkin hatch-broods united by an ember carried from the mint's first furnace. They believe the wishing coin is an ancestor's unhatched egg and the seals are pieces of its shell. Obsidian and molten orange, wing shapes and cracked egg crest; spiky swept silhouettes. Rival: Stormhorn Clan, whose sky relics they believe must keep the egg warm.
- Level 1 **Coal Hatchling** — tiny horned dragon clutching a warm coin. “It is not treasure if it still needs hatching.”
- Level 2 **Cinderwing Guardian** — upright drake knight, ember wings, furnace spear. “Their mint was our nursery before it was their kingdom.”
- Level 3 **Pyrecrest Ascendant** — majestic dragon champion, blazing wing crown, molten egg-shaped shield. “Gather the shell, kin of the ember. Our oldest ancestor is about to wake.”

## Legendary — one family

### 12. Crownless Clan
<!-- family-id: crownless-court -->
Fey coin-kin descended from the first bargain, with no bloodline and no fixed faces. They offer each clan its heart's desire, claiming every wager makes the twelve one family again. Midnight blue and iridescent gold, floating crowns and impossible circles; weightless circular silhouettes. Rival: Hearthshield Clan, who remember the price of their last generous offer; temptation for all eleven other clans.
- Level 1 **Penny Page** — mischievous tiny fey with coin face and hovering empty crown. “They called it luck when we were being polite.”
- Level 2 **Gilded Envoy** — masked fey diplomat, crescent coat, coin constellation. “We offer each clan exactly what its cousins fear.”
- Level 3 **Sovereign of Chances** — elegant faceless fey monarch, orbiting split crown and twin coin moons. “Choose your side. We have already chosen the moment.”

## Art production brief

Use the art creation scripts in the adjacent recard RTG project. Produce 36 distinct square sticker images, one per clan and level. Preserve each clan's palette, emblem and silhouette through all levels. Level 1 looks approachable and modest; level 2 gains equipment and confident posture; level 3 gets a clear dramatic silhouette while remaining legible at mobile card size. Original whimsical fantasy illustration, bold outline, clean negative space, no embedded text or rarity labels. UI renders names, lore and rarity as accessible HTML. Stable family IDs tie the existing illustrations and owned copies to each renamed clan.

## Story review — approved with required acceptance notes

These notes approve the feature stories and creative direction, not an untested interface.

- Show rarity text and “Level 1/2/3” separately; color is supplementary (#1 visibility, #4 consistency).
- Each drop names the family, form and rarity; evolution explicitly explains “3 copies combined” and reveals the new form (#1 visibility).
- Album must show owned count by family/level, locked count and collection progress; never imply a locked copy is selectable (#5 error prevention).
- Wagers are optional. Each player sees both exact form names, levels and copies at risk before accepting. Plain text: “Winner keeps both stickers.” A change to either offer clears prior acceptance (#5 error prevention).
- Pending offers can be declined or cancelled before starting; tied or cancelled matches clearly return stickers (#3 control).
- Transferred/returned copies trigger automatic evolution only after unlocking, with a visible explanation. Locked copies do not count towards evolution (#1, #5).
- Never promise guaranteed drops after two flips: 50% is independent per valid flip. Rarity odds are conditional on receiving a sticker (#2 mental model).
- Mobile choices use visible cards and labeled buttons at least 44px tall; no color-only rarity and no tiny native selector carrying essential wager information (#6 recognition).
